# The sound examples

Every clip here was rendered on a computer from LarkOS's own source code:
the plugins' DSP for the instruments, and the firmware's Beads and Cassette
code for the effects. None was recorded from a Tracker Mini, and none has
been compared with one yet.

**Synths.** Soli, June, Hexa and Ham each play the same four phrases at
100 BPM in C minor, with one factory preset per phrase:

| | Pad | Lead | Bass | Keys |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Soli | Solina | Violin | Synth Bass | String Piano |
| June | Strings | Saw Lead | 106 Bass | Electric Key |
| Hexa | Two Rooms | Sync Runner | Reso Acid | Tine Bloom |
| Ham | Aeolian | Krummhorn | Walking Bass | Jazz Trio |

**Rings.** One clip per model, each playing the same struck arpeggio, with
the plugin's default Structure, Brightness, Damping and Position and two
voices.

**Plaits.** Six engines with their knobs at noon. The wavetable engine's
Harmonics is at 0.2, which picks a factory bank. The FM bass's Decay is at
0.3. The beat uses three voices on the bass drum, snare and hi-hat engines,
with the hats on a quieter track.

**Beads.** June's Electric Key on its own for two bars, then the track's
Beads send goes to 100. Beads is at Size 70, Density 85, Feedback 50 and
Reverb 50, with every other setting at its default.

**Cassette.** The same two bars over x0x's 808 kit, then Cassette comes on
at its default settings.

**Levels.** Each clip is levelled to -18 LUFS, or less where that would take
its true peak above -1.5 dBTP. A 15 Hz high-pass removes any DC offset, and
the ends are faded. The clips are encoded as MP3 (LAME, VBR quality 4).
